Privacy Policy & Telemetry Governance

Effective: October 2025 | Quant Labs Enterprise Production Infrastructure

1. Scope & Purpose

Quant Labs Enterprise ("we", "our", or "the Platform") operates this quantitative backtesting, live paper trading, and model evaluation infrastructure. This Privacy Policy details our operational protocols regarding telemetry collection, cryptographic data handling, and algorithmic telemetry logging.

2. Telemetry & Quantitative Data Collection

Our platform records computational and trading execution metrics strictly necessary for model validation, including: simulated order execution timestamps, entry/exit price fills, unrealized and realized PnL calculations, backtest matrix hyperparameters, and cloud compute resource utilization (CPU, memory, GPU allocation).

3. Cryptographic Storage & Infrastructure Security

All sensitive credentials, API keys, and database connections are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM and managed via Google Cloud Secret Manager and Cloud KMS. No plaintext secrets are recorded in persistent browser storage or unencrypted disk volumes. Real-time log streams are transmitted over secure TLS 1.3 channels.

4. Third-Party Integrations & Cloud Providers

Platform operations interface with enterprise infrastructure providers including Google Cloud Platform (GCP compute engines, Cloud SQL, and Cloud Storage) and public digital asset exchange WebSocket feeds (Binance) exclusively for live price tick ingestion. No proprietary strategy models or client trading weights are shared with third-party aggregators.

5. Data Retention & Audit Logs

Audit logs of simulated executions, model hyperparameters, and automated health checks are preserved in immutable cloud buckets for quantitative validation and regulatory audit compliance for up to 24 months, after which automated lifecycle pruning policies apply.

6. Inquiries & Contact

For questions regarding security governance, algorithmic logging, or platform access controls, contact the Quant Infrastructure Team at security@quantlabs.cloud or call our 24/7 Operations Support Desk at +1 (800) 555-0199.
